# Travel Policy: Booking Business Travel

**Lakeshore Analytics Inc.** · Policy owner: Finance · Version 2.1 · Effective 1 February 2026

This policy explains how to book business travel, which class of travel you may use, and who approves a trip.

**Spending limits are not in this policy.** Limits for meals, hotels and taxis are set in the separate expense policy, which also gives the higher limits for some cities.

## 1. How to book

- All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through **TripDesk**, the company travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere will not be reimbursed unless TripDesk could not offer the trip.
- Book flights at least **14 days before departure**. For bookings made later than this, TripDesk asks for a short reason, which your approver will see.
- Choose the lowest fare that fits your schedule. Fully flexible fares are allowed only when the trip dates are likely to change.

## 2. Class of travel

### Flights

| Scheduled flight time (one way) | Class allowed |
|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 to 10 hours | **Premium economy** |
| Over 10 hours | **Business class, with approval from a Vice President** |

- Flight time is the scheduled time of the longest single flight on the journey, not the total journey time with connections.
- You may pay personally to upgrade, but the company will not reimburse the difference.

### Trains

- Standard class for journeys under 3 hours; first class for longer journeys.

## 3. Approvals

- **Domestic trips** are approved by your **line manager**.
- **International trips** are approved by your **director**, and must be approved before anything is booked.
- Business-class flights also need **Vice President** approval, as in section 2.

## 4. Travel insurance and safety

- Company travel insurance covers all approved business trips. You do not need to buy your own.
- Before an international trip, check the travel advice for your destination in TripDesk. Trips to countries rated "high risk" need approval from the Security team as well as your director.

## 5. Combining business and personal travel

- You may add personal days to a business trip, as long as the flight cost does not increase. Any extra cost is yours.
- Hotel nights for personal days are not reimbursed.

## 6. Questions

Contact the Finance travel team through TripDesk.
