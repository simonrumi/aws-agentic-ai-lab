# IT Policy: Equipment and Access

**Lakeshore Analytics Inc.** · Policy owner: IT Service Desk · Version 3.0 · Effective 1 March 2026

This policy covers company laptops, phones, software, and how employees sign in to company systems.

## 1. Laptops

- Every employee receives one company laptop on their first day.
- Laptops are replaced on a **42-month cycle** (three and a half years) from the date they were issued. The IT Service Desk contacts you when your replacement is due; you do not need to request it.
- An early replacement is possible only if the laptop is faulty and cannot be repaired, or if your role changes and needs a higher specification. Raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk.
- Do not install a different operating system or remove company security software.

### Lost or stolen equipment

- Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the **Security Hotline (extension 4357)** within **4 hours** of noticing it is missing, at any time of day.
- The device will be locked and wiped remotely. Also report the loss to the police if it was stolen, and give the IT Service Desk the reference number.

## 2. Phones

- Company phones are provided to roles that are on call or travel more than 4 times a year.
- Other employees may use a personal phone for work email and chat once it is enrolled in the company's device management app.

## 3. Software

- Software on the **Approved Software Catalogue** can be installed from the self-service portal without approval.
- Software not in the catalogue must be requested through an IT Service Desk ticket. The IT Security team reviews every new request.
- If the software costs more than **$200 a year** per user, your **department head** must also approve the cost before it is bought.
- Browser extensions follow the same rules as other software.

## 4. Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long. Use a passphrase of several unrelated words.
- Passwords are **not changed on a schedule**. Change yours immediately if you think it may have been seen or stolen, and tell the IT Service Desk.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every company system. Use the company authenticator app; text-message codes are not allowed.
- Never share your password, including with the IT Service Desk. IT staff will never ask for it.

## 5. Access to systems and data

- New access is requested through an IT Service Desk ticket and approved by the **owner of the system**, not by your line manager.
- Access is reviewed every 6 months. Access that has not been used in 90 days is removed automatically.
- When you leave the company, all access is removed on your last working day.

## 6. Questions

Raise a ticket in the IT Service Desk portal, or call extension 4000 during office hours.
