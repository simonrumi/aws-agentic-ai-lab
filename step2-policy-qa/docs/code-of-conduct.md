# Code of Conduct

**Lakeshore Analytics Inc.** · Policy owner: Legal and Compliance · Version 5.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

Our code of conduct sets out how we expect everyone at Lakeshore to behave: towards each other, our clients and the public. It applies to all employees, contractors and board members.

## 1. Respect at work

- Treat colleagues, clients and suppliers with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Raise problems early and in good faith.

## 2. Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept a gift or hospitality from a client or supplier if it is worth **$75 or less** and is not offered in exchange for a decision.
- Anything worth **more than $75** must be declined, or recorded in the **Gifts Register** within **5 working days**. Legal and Compliance will decide whether it can be kept.
- Never accept cash, gift cards or vouchers, whatever the value.
- Never accept anything from a supplier while a tender or contract decision involving them is in progress.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

- A conflict of interest arises when your personal interests could affect, or seem to affect, your decisions at work. Examples: a family member works for a supplier; you hold shares in a competitor.
- Every employee completes the annual **conflict-of-interest declaration by 31 January**, even if they have nothing to declare.
- If a new conflict arises during the year, declare it to Legal and Compliance within 10 working days.

## 4. Outside work

- You may take on paid work outside Lakeshore only with written approval from your line manager, and only if it does not compete with Lakeshore or use company time or equipment.

## 5. Confidential information

- Client data and company plans are confidential. Share them only with people who need them for their work.
- Do not paste client data or confidential company information into public AI tools or websites.

## 6. Speaking up

- If you see something that breaks this code, raise it with your line manager or with Legal and Compliance.
- You can also use the **Speak Up line**, which is run by an independent company and lets you report **anonymously**, by phone or online, at any time.
- Lakeshore does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who speaks up in good faith.

## 7. Breaking the code

Breaking this code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
