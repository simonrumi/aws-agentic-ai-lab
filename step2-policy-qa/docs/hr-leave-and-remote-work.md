# HR Policy: Leave and Remote Work

**Lakeshore Analytics Inc.** · Policy owner: People Team · Version 4.2 · Effective 1 January 2026

This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors should follow the terms of their own contract.

## 1. Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive **23 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.
- Leave must be requested in the HR portal and approved by your line manager before it is taken.
- Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days need at least 4 weeks' notice.

### Carrying leave over

- You may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; any left after that date are lost.
- Unused days beyond the 5-day limit are not paid out, except when you leave the company (see section 5).

## 2. Sick leave

- Tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day you are unwell.
- For absences of up to 5 working days, you can self-certify in the HR portal.
- For absences longer than 5 working days, a doctor's note is required.

## 3. Parental leave

- The primary carer receives **18 weeks** of leave at full pay, followed by up to 8 further weeks unpaid.
- The secondary carer receives 4 weeks of leave at full pay.
- Parental leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

## 4. Remote work

Lakeshore runs a hybrid model. The aim is to keep the benefits of focused home working while giving teams regular time together.

- Employees may work remotely for up to **2 days a week**.
- **Tuesday is the company-wide office day.** Everyone whose role is office-based is expected on site on Tuesdays, unless they are on leave or travelling for work.
- Remote days are agreed with your line manager and recorded in the HR portal. They do not carry over to later weeks.
- Fully remote arrangements are by exception only and need approval from your department head and the People Team.

### Working from another country

- You may work from outside your home country for up to **15 working days** in a calendar year.
- You must get approval from the People Team at least 2 weeks in advance, so that tax and visa rules can be checked.

## 5. Leaving the company

### Notice periods

| Role | Notice you give | Notice the company gives |
|---|---|---|
| Individual contributors | **6 weeks** | 6 weeks |
| People managers and above | **3 months** | 3 months |
| During probation (first 6 months) | 1 week | 1 week |

- Notice must be given in writing to your line manager, copied to the People Team.
- Accrued but unused annual leave is paid out in your final salary payment.

## 6. Questions

Contact the People Team through the HR portal. This policy is reviewed every year in November.
